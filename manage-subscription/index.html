Manage or cancel your subscription

Pocket Lyrics Pro is billed by Google Play or the App Store, so you manage it there. Cancelling stops future charges; Pro stays until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Android (Google Play)

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture at the top right.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  4. Tap Pocket Lyrics.
  5. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the steps. To switch between monthly and yearly, choose the other plan here instead.

Or open play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions signed in with the Google account you subscribed with.

iPhone and iPad (App Store)

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap Pocket Lyrics.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription, then confirm. To switch between monthly and yearly, choose the other option here instead.

Or, on your iPhone or iPad, open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

From inside Pocket Lyrics

Open Settings (the gear at the top right) and tap Manage subscription. It takes you to the right page in Google Play or the App Store.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. The subscription belongs to your Google Play or App Store account, so cancel it there first, or the store will keep charging you.

Free trial

New subscribers get a 3-day free trial. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. You can keep using Pro until the trial is over.

Refunds

Payments are taken by Google or Apple, and refunds are handled by them under their own policies. For Google Play, see Google Play refunds; for the App Store, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
