Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Email support@goodmarklabs.com.

Getting started

What is Pocket Lyrics?

A chords and lyrics songbook for your phone and tablet. You add the songs you play, with the chords above the words, and read them on stage. It also has set lists, transpose and capo, auto-scroll, chord detection, a chords library and a tuner.

Does it come with songs?

No. Pocket Lyrics never downloads lyrics or chords from the internet. You type or paste in your own songs, or import files you already have.

How do I add a song?

Tap + on the Songs tab, then type or paste your song with each chord line directly above its lyric line, like a chord sheet. Pocket Lyrics keeps the chords lined up with the words.

Can I import songs I already have?

Yes. Pocket Lyrics imports ChordPro (.cho) files and plain text files with chords written above the lyrics. Go to Settings > Import & export.

Is it on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Pocket Lyrics is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android.

Does it work in languages other than English?

Yes. Songs can be in any language and script, and chords are placed so they never split a character.

Playing

Does it work offline?

Yes. Each device keeps a copy of your songbook, so you can open songs, read charts and edit with no connection. Anything you change is synced when you are back online. The tuner and chord detection also run entirely on the device.

How does transpose work?

Use − and + on the reading screen to move the song to a new key. It only changes what you see, so the original stays safe. Tap Save in this key if you want to keep the new key.

What does the capo setting do?

Set a capo and the chords show the shapes to hold, while the song still sounds in its key. Suggest capo finds the easiest open-chord shapes for the song.

Is it good for worship teams?

Yes. Build a set list for each service in playing order, choose a key for each song without changing the song itself, and share the set as a PDF (Pro) or ChordPro file with the band.

How accurate is Detect?

Detect is a helpful starting point, not a replacement for your ears. It works best when the instruments are easy to hear and can be wrong with busy music or in a noisy room. Sound is processed on your phone and never recorded or uploaded.

Account and sync

How does sync work?

Your songs and sets are saved to your account, so signing in on another phone or tablet brings them across. Changes you make on one device appear on the others when they next connect.

Can I get my songs out again?

At any time. Settings > Import & export > Export all songs gives you a ChordPro file containing your whole library, which you can open in any text editor or import into another app.

Can other people see my songs?

No. Your songs and sets are private to your account. There are no ads, analytics or trackers. The Privacy Policy has the full picture.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings > Delete account. This permanently removes your account, songs and sets. If you cannot open the app, email support@goodmarklabs.com from your account’s email address. Full instructions are on the delete account page.

Pro and billing

What do I get with Pro?

Unlimited songs, Detect, chord charts (the Chords library and chord diagrams in your songs) and PDF export. Everything else, including set lists, transpose, capo, auto-scroll and the tuner, is free. See Pricing.

How much is Pro?

US$2.99 a month or US$19.99 a year, with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. Prices may vary by country and are shown in your local currency in the App Store and Google Play.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New subscribers get a 3-day free trial. If you don’t cancel before it ends, the subscription starts and the store charges you. The store decides who qualifies, normally once per store account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Google Play or your Apple ID settings, or from the app at Settings > Manage subscription. Cancelling stops future charges, and Pro stays until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Step-by-step: Manage or cancel your subscription.

Does deleting my account cancel my subscription?

No. The subscription belongs to your App Store or Google Play account, so cancel it there first, or you will keep being charged.

What happens to my songs if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted or hidden. You can still read, play and export every song. While you have more than 10 songs you can’t add new ones, and the Pro tools lock until you subscribe again.

It says my subscription is linked to another account.

Pro stays with the Pocket Lyrics account you subscribed on. Sign in with that account, or email support@goodmarklabs.com from the account you are using now and we will move it for you.

How do refunds work?

Payments go through Google Play or the App Store, so refunds are handled by them under their own policies.
