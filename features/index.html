Features

Everything a working musician needs to keep their songs ready, and nothing that gets in the way.

Songs & chords

Chords right above the words

Type or paste a song with the chords on the line above the lyrics, just like a chord sheet. Pocket Lyrics keeps each chord over the syllable it belongs to, at any text size and on any screen.

  • Search by title, artist or lyrics; sort A–Z or by recent.
  • Pinch to zoom the text on the reading screen.
  • Songs in any language and script.
  • Import ChordPro (.cho) and plain-text files; export your whole library any time.
Transpose & capo

Play it in the key that suits the room

Transpose chords up or down in a tap. The view changes and your original stays as it is, unless you choose Save in this key. Set a capo and the chords show the shapes to hold, while the song keeps sounding in its key.

  • Suggest capo picks the easiest open-chord shapes.
  • Optionally show the sounding chord too, like “D (Eb)”.
  • A set can keep its own key and capo for each song.
Auto-scroll

Hands on the instrument, not the screen

Start auto-scroll and the song moves at its own pace. Make it a little faster or slower and Pocket Lyrics remembers the speed for that song, on every device.

  • Tap to pause or resume; a drag pauses it too.
  • It stops at the end of the song.
  • The same pace whatever your text size or screen.
Set lists

Set lists for Sunday, rehearsal and the gig

Build a set list in playing order and play straight through it. Sets point to your songs rather than copying them, so fixing a chord once fixes it everywhere.

  • Pick a key for each song in the set without changing the song.
  • Drag to reorder.
  • Share a set as a ChordPro file, or as a PDF with Pro.
Detect Pro

Hear a chord, see its name

Play a chord on your guitar or keyboard and Pocket Lyrics names it. Song mode follows recorded music, or works out the chords and the key of an MP3 or M4A file you choose. Everything is processed on your phone; nothing is recorded or uploaded.

  • Major, minor and 7th chords in instrument mode.
  • Song mode suggests the key, too.
  • A helpful starting point; it works best when the instruments are easy to hear.
Chords library Pro

Every chord shape, for guitar and keyboard

Look up any chord by name or pick a root and a type. See guitar shapes, with several positions to choose from, or the notes on a keyboard. In a song, tap a chord to see how to play it.

  • 40 chord types, from major and minor to sus, add9 and power chords.
  • Guitar diagrams with finger numbers.
  • Keyboard diagrams with the chord’s notes highlighted.
Tuner

Tune up without leaving the app

The built-in guitar tuner listens only while it’s on screen. Play a string and it shows whether you’re flat or sharp, with a soft chime when it’s in tune.

  • Standard, drop D and half step down.
  • Auto string detection, or tap a string to choose it.
  • Free for everyone.

And the essentials

  • Works offline

    Your songs live on your device, so they open with no connection. Changes sync when you're back online.

  • Sync

    Sign in on your phone and tablet and your songbook stays the same on both.

  • PDF export Pro

    Save or share a song or a whole set as a clean PDF with the chords in place.

  • Any language

    Songs can be in any script, and chords never split a character.

  • Private by design

    No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Only you see your songs.

  • Your songs, your words

    Pocket Lyrics never downloads lyrics or chords. Everything in your library is what you put there.
