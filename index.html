Chords and lyrics app

Your songbook, in your key, on every stage.

Pocket Lyrics keeps the songs you play on your phone or tablet, with the chords sitting right above the words. Transpose in a tap, build set lists for Sunday or the gig, and let the page scroll while you play.

Free for up to 10 songs. Pro is US$2.99/month or US$19.99/year, with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. Prices may vary by country. You’ll see your local price in the app before you subscribe.

Meet Pocket Lyrics

The video plays from YouTube. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play.

Detect Pro

Hear a chord. Know it instantly.

Play a chord on your guitar or keyboard and Pocket Lyrics names it, shows how sure it is, and what else it could be. Tap the chord to see how to play it.

  • Song mode follows recorded music and suggests the key.
  • Voice mode: sing or hum a song and see the possible keys.
  • Processed on your phone. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • Part of Pocket Lyrics Pro, with a 3-day free trial.

More about Detect

Made for the people who play the songs

Worship teams, gigging musicians and anyone learning their favourite songs. Simple enough to hand to someone with no instructions.

  • Chords over lyrics

    Type a chord line above its lyric line, the way you'd write it on paper. The chords stay above the right syllable at any text size.

  • Transpose and capo

    Change key in a tap, or set a capo and see the shapes to hold. Suggest capo finds the easiest open chords.

  • Auto-scroll

    The song scrolls at its own pace so your hands stay on the instrument. Tap to pause.

  • Set lists

    Put songs in playing order for a service, rehearsal or gig, with a key for each song.

  • Detect Pro

    Play a chord and Pocket Lyrics names it, or let it follow a recording and write the chords down.

  • Tuner

    A guitar tuner built in, with standard, drop D and half-step-down tunings.

  • Voice mode Pro

    Sing or hum a song and see the possible keys. Part of Detect.

  • Open ChordPro files

    Open ChordPro (.cho) files from Files, email or chat, and add them to your songbook.

  • Share several songs

    Pick several songs and share them at once as PDF, ChordPro or text. PDF is part of Pro.

On stage

A clean page when you're playing

The reading screen is just your song. The controls tuck themselves away while you perform and come back with a tap.

  • Works fully offline. No signal needed on stage.
  • Your songs sync between your phone and tablet.
  • Import and export ChordPro files, so your songs are never locked in.
