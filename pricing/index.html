Pricing

Start free. Upgrade to Pro when your songbook grows or you want the extra tools.

Free

NZ$0

Free to download and use.

  • Up to 10 songs (imported songs count too)
  • Set lists
  • Transpose, capo and text size
  • Auto-scroll
  • Guitar tuner
  • Import and export ChordPro files
  • Sync and offline use

No card needed.

Pro 3-day free trial

NZ$4.99 / month

or NZ$34.99 / year

  • Everything in Free
  • Unlimited songs
  • Detect: name the chord you play, or follow a whole song
  • Chord charts: the Chords library and chord diagrams in your songs
  • Save and share songs and sets as PDF

New subscribers get a 3-day free trial. Subscribe in the app; you can cancel any time in Google Play or the App Store.

Good to know

  • Prices are in New Zealand dollars. The App Store and Google Play show the price in your own currency before you buy, and it can differ between countries.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. How to manage or cancel.
  • If Pro ends, nothing is deleted or hidden. You can still read, play and export every song; adding new songs is paused while you have more than 10.
  • Pro stays with the Pocket Lyrics account you subscribed on, and unlocks on every device you sign in to.
